2025/07/06 [Sun]doodle #021 posted + site relaunchNEW!
big week. doodle #021 is up in the art gallery (the coffee ghost finally got a body), and i rebuilt this whole site as actual separate pages with real links between them — the old version was one big file pretending to be a website, and it showed. it finally behaves like the 90s taught us: click a tab, get a page.
2025/06/29 [Sun]finished "rainy save point"
it took way too long but the ending finally breathes. lesson of the week: rests are a feature, not a failure — cut 30% of the notes and it got better immediately. it's playable on the music page now. slow track, headphones, maybe tea.
2025/06/22 [Sun]redraw challenge day 12
pulled out my 2019 sketchbook to redraw one old piece side by side (the blob cat, obviously). the lines are less defensive now — shoulders stopped being ellipses i was apologizing for. added a before/after slider to the art page so you can watch it happen.
2025/06/15 [Sun]palette purge!!
deleted 40 unused swatches and felt a small violent joy. keeping 18 colors now, including two that exist only so i feel "artistic". rule going forward: if you didn't use it in 3 months, it goes to the attic file.
2025/06/08 [Sun]played my first demo for a friend
made someone listen to the 2019 track on purpose. we both winced at second four. they said "you can actually hear your taste now", which is the nicest way i know to say progress. kept it in the tracklist anyway — labeled, preserved, slightly ashamed.
